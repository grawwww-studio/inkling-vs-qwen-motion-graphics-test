The root cause: the display font is far wider than the brief's estimate (~120px/letter at 150px, not 100px), so the single-line title overflows the canvas and the boxes collide. The clean fix is to split the title onto two lines — each word gets its own generous box, nothing crosses the edge, and the stagger/kinetic feel is preserved.
